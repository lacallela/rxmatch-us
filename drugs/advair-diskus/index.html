FDA Clinical & Prescribing Information: Advair Diskus

FDA Indications & Uses

Treatment of asthma in patients aged 4 years and older; maintenance treatment of COPD.

Dosage & Administration

Inhale 1 blister twice daily approximately 12 hours apart. Rinse mouth with water after inhalation.

Contraindications & Warnings

Primary treatment of status asthmaticus; severe milk protein allergy.

Common Adverse Effects

Oral thrush, hoarseness, throat irritation, headache.
